About the Shop

A Scottsville Staple, One Chair at a Time

Corner Barber Shop is owned and operated by Jessica, a skilled barber who brings the same care and integrity to every haircut that her family has brought to this community for generations. Located along South Constitution Route — right next door to Scottsville Power Equipment — the shop has become a welcoming stop for locals and travelers alike.

Whether you're a regular from down the road or passing through on the way to visit family in southern Virginia, you'll find a comfortable chair, attentive service, and a haircut done your way — not just copied from a style on the wall.

Jessica listens first, cuts second. That's the difference between a quick trim and a cut you're happy to walk out with.
