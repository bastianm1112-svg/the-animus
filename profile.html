This isn't a casual political preference but an expression of your deepest philosophical commitments: the Nietzschean emphasis on individual self-determination, rational egoism's insistence on personal sovereignty, and the libertarian moral foundation's treatment of coercion as the primary evil. The combination of high economic freedom and strong libertarian orientation is internally coherent with your Ti-dominant, Fi-present cognitive profile — you reason your way to principles and hold them independently of social consensus.

Philosophical Roots
Your Ti-dominant cognitive stack means you derive political positions from first principles rather than tribal affiliation. The rational egoist ethical framework directly implies property rights and anti-coercion as foundational. Your Nietzschean philosophy rejects the herd morality that underlies collectivist politics. Interestingly, your — Enneagram creates a tension: the Type 1's principled moral rigidity sometimes conflicts with the libertarian's reluctance to impose standards on others.
Thinkers & Tensions
Your position aligns with Ayn Rand's Objectivism, Friedrich Hayek's spontaneous order, and elements of classical liberalism. The tension in your profile: your Type 1 perfectionism and high moral foundations scores in Fairness/Liberty want things to be right — which can pull toward wanting to enforce certain standards, in conflict with the libertarian principle of non-interference. This is the core unresolved tension of the Nietzschean-libertarian: personal excellence as imperative vs. the non-judgmental stance the libertarian framework logically demands.
